# Responsible research starter kit

Use this framework only against mock or explicitly authorized targets. Begin
with the synthetic workflow in [REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md); it
needs no device and produces no third-party traffic.

## Before a campaign

Record: target owner and authorization, intended input surface, scope,
stop conditions, data-handling plan, and disclosure contact. Do not run a
campaign when these details are unknown.

## Evidence and disclosure

Keep the reproducibility record, minimized input, output, and impact evidence
private until the affected party agrees otherwise. Public reports should use
synthetic examples unless disclosure has been coordinated. A crash alone is not
proof of exploitability or eligibility for a bounty.

## Non-goals

Do not use this project for surveillance, persistence, credential theft,
permission bypass, exploitation of third-party systems, or publishing private
research material. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
