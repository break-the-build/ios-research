# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone. Participate in good faith, assume positive intent, and keep criticism
focused on ideas and work rather than people.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.
- Respect authorization boundaries, coordinated disclosure, and privacy.
- Share only material you are entitled to share; redact sensitive target,
  researcher, and vulnerability details.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private research
details without permission, or requests to weaken this project's safety
boundary are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content, warn participants,
or restrict participation as needed to protect the community. Retaliation
against a reporter is prohibited.
